Privacy Policy — Lockout

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Lockout is designed with privacy at its core. In plain terms: nothing you do in Lockout ever leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing is sent anywhere. Lockout has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The app makes no internet connection at all. Lockout collects no personal data and shares none.

The only information Lockout stores is kept locally on your own device:

The Bluetooth address of the tag you pair, so it knows which tag is yours.
The time your tag was last seen, so the guard can work correctly.
Any "safe place" you set up, including its GPS coordinates and Wi-Fi network details, so the app can recognise when you are somewhere you've marked as safe.

All of this is stored only on your phone. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared, and it is removed if you uninstall the app.

Permissions and why they're needed

Lockout asks for a few Android permissions purely so it can do its job:

Bluetooth / Nearby devices — to detect your paired tag.
Location, including in the background ("Allow all the time") — Android requires location permission for any app that scans for Bluetooth devices. Lockout scans for your tag even while your screen is off, so that it can detect your tag returning and automatically re-arm the lock. This background scanning is why Android classes the permission as background location. Lockout does not track, record, store, or share your location for this purpose — it uses the permission solely to detect your tag.
Location data for "safe places" — when you choose to create a safe place, Lockout records that location's GPS coordinates and nearby Wi-Fi details on your device only, so it can recognise when you are somewhere you've marked as safe. This information never leaves your phone.
Device administrator — Lockout uses this permission only to lock your device as part of its anti-theft function. It is not used for anything else.
Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data, because we never collect any off your device in the first place.

Data storage and security

All Lockout data is stored locally on your device. Because none of it is ever sent off your phone, there is no server-side database to breach. Uninstalling the app removes all data it has stored.

Children

Lockout is a general-purpose security tool and is not directed at children.
