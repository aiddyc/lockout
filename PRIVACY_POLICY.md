# Privacy Policy — Lockout

**Last updated: 8 August 2026**

Lockout is designed with privacy at its core. In plain terms: nothing you do in Lockout ever leaves your phone.

## Who provides this app

Lockout is developed and published by TagR ("we", "us"). You can reach us at lockoutbytagr@gmail.com. Because Lockout processes all data locally on your device and transmits nothing to us or anyone else, we do not hold, receive, or control any of your personal data.

## What we collect

Nothing is sent anywhere. Lockout has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The app makes no internet connection at all. Lockout does not transmit or share any personal data. Any data it uses is processed solely on your device.

The only information Lockout stores is kept locally on your own device:

- The Bluetooth address of the tag you pair, so it knows which tag is yours.
- The time your tag was last seen, so the guard can work correctly.
- Any "safe place" you set up, including its GPS coordinates and Wi-Fi network details, so the app can recognise when you are somewhere you've marked as safe.

All of this is stored only on your phone. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared, and it is removed if you uninstall the app.

## Permissions and why they're needed

Lockout asks for a few Android permissions purely so it can do its job:

- **Bluetooth / Nearby devices** — to detect your paired tag.
- **Location, including in the background ("Allow all the time")** — Lockout's core anti-theft feature detects your paired tag and automatically locks your phone when the tag leaves range, even when the app is closed or not in use. Lockout scans for your tag even while your screen is off, so it can detect your tag returning and automatically re-arm the lock. Android classes the permission needed for this background Bluetooth scanning as background location. Lockout does not track, record, store, or share your location — it uses the permission solely to detect your tag on your device.
- **Location data for "safe places"** — when you choose to create a safe place, Lockout records that location's GPS coordinates and nearby Wi-Fi details on your device only, so it can recognise when you are somewhere you've marked as safe. This information never leaves your phone.
- **Device administrator** — Lockout uses this permission only to lock your device as part of its anti-theft function. It is not used for anything else.

## Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data, because nothing is ever collected off your device in the first place.

## Data storage and security

All Lockout data is stored locally on your device. Because none of it is ever sent off your phone, there is no server-side database to breach. Uninstalling the app removes all data it has stored.

## Your rights

Because all processing happens on your device and no data is transmitted to us, we do not hold any personal data about you to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control: you can clear the app's data or uninstall it at any time to remove everything it has stored, and you can change or revoke any permission in your device settings.

## Children

Lockout is a general-purpose security tool and is not directed at children.
