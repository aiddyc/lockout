# Lockout

**Your phone locks itself the moment it leaves your side.**

Lockout is an Android app that pairs with the Bluetooth tag included in your kit. If your phone is ever snatched or left behind, it locks automatically within about two seconds of losing range — before anyone can use it.

---

## 📥 Download the app

**[⬇️ Download the latest version of Lockout](https://github.com/aiddyc/lockout/releases/latest)**

Tap the link above on your Android phone, then open the file ending in **`.apk`** to install.

---

## 📱 Installing (first time only)

Because Lockout is installed directly rather than from the Play Store, your phone will ask for permission the first time:

1. Tap the downloaded **`.apk`** file (you'll find it in your **Downloads**).
2. If a message says *"For your security, your phone can't install unknown apps from this source,"* tap **Settings**, then switch on **Allow from this source**, and go back.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

This is completely normal for apps installed outside the Play Store — you only do it once.

---

## ⚙️ Setting up Lockout

For Lockout to protect your phone, complete these steps once:

1. **Turn on your tag by squeezing the center of the tag until it flashes green** (pull the small plastic tab out of the battery if it's new).
2. Open Lockout and tap **Scan for tags**, then tap your TagR when it appears to pair it.
3. **Grant the permissions** the app asks for. These are what let the guard work:
   - **Bluetooth / Nearby devices** — so the app can detect your tag.
   - **Location: set to *Precise*** — needed for Android to allow tag detection.
   - **Location: set to *Allow all the time*** — so the guard keeps working with the screen off.

   > 👉 Choosing *Approximate* location or *Only while using the app* will stop the guard working, so please pick **Precise** and **Allow all the time**.

4. Switch the **Guard** on. That's it — your phone is now protected.

**Optional:** set up a **safe place** (like your home) so Lockout won't lock the phone while you're there with the tag.

---

## ✅ Requirements

- **Android only** — Lockout does not work on iPhone/iOS.
- Android 8.0 or newer.
- The Bluetooth tag included in your kit.
